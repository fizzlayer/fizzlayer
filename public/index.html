03 / Grounded in your world

A little more awareness.
A lot more peace of mind.

From the server closet to the greenhouse, give Fizz a sensor and something to watch for.

WATER · FLOW & LEAKS

Catch the little leaks.

See unexpected water flow before a small problem becomes a big one. Set a threshold and let Fizz keep watch.

“Was there any water flow overnight?”

TEMPERATURE · HEAT & COLD

Keep your cool.

Watch the server closet or cold room from anywhere. Get a call when the temperature crosses your limit.

“How warm is the server closet?”

HUMIDITY · GROWING CONDITIONS

Give your plants a voice.

Keep an eye on the air in your greenhouse and set an alert when humidity drops below your chosen level.

“Is the greenhouse too dry?”

GAS · AIR QUALITY

Know what’s in the air.

Track concentration readings in your garage or workshop. Add context and history alongside your existing detectors.

“Did the garage air spike today?”

SOUND · ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS

Hear the changes.

Monitor sound levels in the spaces that matter. Let Fizz tell you when the noise crosses a threshold.

“Has it been quiet in the pet room?”

PHONE · NO EXTRA HARDWARE

Start with your pocket.

Turn your phone into a sensor for motion, location, and voice notes. Each permission stays in your hands.

“What did my phone pick up?”

Also supports pressure, radio signal strength, and custom metrics over HTTPS.
