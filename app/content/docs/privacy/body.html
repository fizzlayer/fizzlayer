The short version

  • We collect what you give Fizzlayer: your account details, your gateway settings, and the MCP server credentials you store, which we encrypt.
  • Tool calls pass through your gateway to your MCP servers. We keep a log of each call's tool and decision, not its argument values or results, for as long as your plan's audit period.
  • We use your data only to run Fizzlayer. We do not sell it or show you ads.
  • A few services help us run Fizzlayer. They are listed below.
  • Email privacy@fizzlayer.com and we will delete your data.

Who we are

Fizzlayer (fizzlayer.com) runs an MCP gateway: a service that sits between your AI agents and the MCP servers they use, and checks each tool call against your rules. It is run by Nisarga P, an individual developer, referred to here as “we” or “us”.

This policy covers the Fizzlayer website, the app, the API, and the hosted gateway. It also covers what our API receives when a gateway you run yourself connects to it. A gateway you run without connecting to us sends us nothing.

Fizzlayer used to offer a Sensors product for physical sensors. If your account still holds data from it, this policy covers that data too. Email us if you want it deleted.

What we collect

When you contact sales

  • Your work email, your company name, and any note you write.

When you create an account

  • With email: your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash, never the password itself.
  • With Google: your Google account ID, email address, name, and profile picture link. We use your email address to suggest your Fizzlayer username.
  • With GitHub: your GitHub account ID, username, email address, name, and profile picture link. We use your email address to suggest your Fizzlayer username.
  • Passkeys: if you add one, we store only its public key. Your fingerprint, face, or device PIN never leaves your device.
  • If you signed up with an invite code before sign-up opened, the code you used.

When you subscribe to a paid plan

  • Your plan, its status, and when it renews or ends, along with the Stripe customer and subscription IDs that link your account to Stripe. To create your Stripe customer, we send Stripe your email address and your Fizzlayer account ID.
  • Stripe collects your payment details, such as your card and billing address. The payment form on our checkout page is Stripe's embedded checkout, loaded from Stripe, and what you type in it goes to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

When you set up a gateway

  • Gateways: the name, mode, region, and public URL of each gateway.
  • MCP servers: each server's name, URL or command and arguments, and how it signs in.
  • Server credentials: an API key you give for a server is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is stored. The encryption key is kept with our API, not in the database. We decrypt a key only to build the config your gateway runs, or to list that server's tools when you ask. The app never shows a stored key back to you.
  • Rules and pins: your rules, and for each tool a hash of its name, description, and input schema. When a tool's description changes, we store up to 200 characters of the new description so you can review it.
  • Tokens: for each client token, its name, ID, expiry, and when it was revoked. The token itself is shown to you once and not stored. Registration tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Self-hosted gateways in linked mode: the config revision your gateway applied and a short status message it reports.

When your agents use the gateway

  • Tool calls: requests and responses pass through the gateway to the MCP servers you added. We do not store argument values or tool results.
  • Audit log: for each tool call, the gateway, server, and tool, your user ID and the ID of the token used, a one-way hash of the arguments, the names of the argument fields, the decision, the reason, and the time.
  • Usage: daily counts of tool calls and approvals for each gateway.
  • Approvals: for each call that waits for approval, the server, tool, argument hash, argument field names, its status, who decided it, and when it expires.
  • Logs: the gateway process writes a line for each decision with the tool, the server, and the decision. Our hosting providers keep standard request logs.

When you use phone approvals

  • The US phone number you pair. Pairing works by calling that number and reading a code. The code is stored only as a hash, expires in about 10 minutes, and works once.
  • A record of each approval call: the number called, whether it was placed, and when. The call reads out the gateway name, the tool name, the argument field names, and a short approval ID. We turn off call recording.

When you search the server catalog

Our server sends your search text to the official MCP Registry (registry.modelcontextprotocol.io) to find matching servers. It does not send your name, email address, or account ID with it.

Collected automatically

  • IP address: used to stop abuse of sign-up and sign-in with rate limits. We store it only as a one-way hash, and delete it within about two days.
  • Server logs: our hosting providers keep standard request logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for a short time.
  • Page views: we count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which records the page address (without anything after a ? or #), the referring site, your country and region, and your browser, operating system, and device type. It uses no cookies. Visitors are told apart by a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours.

We do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To run Fizzlayer: build and serve your gateway's config, check each tool call against your rules, show you approvals and tool changes, and place approval calls.
  • To enforce plan limits and bill paid plans.
  • To keep Fizzlayer secure and stop abuse.
  • To reply to your sales inquiry, and to email you about important changes to Fizzlayer.
  • To fix problems and improve Fizzlayer.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Who helps us run Fizzlayer

These services process data for us, only to provide their part of Fizzlayer:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in, and runs the hosted gateway process. Stores your account and gateway data.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and API, carries traffic to the hosted gateway, and counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics.
  • Stripe: processes payments for paid plans and stores your payment details.
  • AgentPhone: places approval calls, and the pairing call that reads your code, to a number you asked us to call. Recording is off.
  • Google: handles Google sign-in.
  • GitHub: handles GitHub sign-in, and hosts the source code you download to run the gateway yourself.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of Fizzlayer. If that happens, this policy will continue to apply to your data.

MCP servers you add receive the tool calls your agents send through the gateway, along with any credential you stored for them. AI agents you connect can call your gateway with the client token you gave them. What those servers and agents do with your data is governed by whoever runs them. You can remove a server or revoke a token at any time in the app.

Google and GitHub sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your basic profile: your Google account ID, email address, name, and profile picture link. We use it only to sign you in and to name your account. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, files, or any other Google data.

Fizzlayer’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If you sign in with GitHub, we receive only your public profile and email address: your GitHub account ID, username, email address, name, and profile picture link. We use it only to sign you in and to name your account. We do not access your repositories, organizations, or any other GitHub data.

Cookies and browser storage

Fizzlayer uses only the cookies it needs to work, and none for analytics:

  • fizz_session keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours.
  • fizz_oauth protects Google and GitHub sign-in and lasts at most 10 minutes.
  • fizz_supabase holds your sign-in for up to 1 hour after you sign in. The app uses it to manage your gateways and to add a passkey.

The app at /app uses Supabase's sign-in library, which can keep your sign-in session in your browser's local storage until you sign out. The checkout page loads Stripe's code, which can set Stripe's own cookies to help prevent fraud. Stripe's privacy policy covers those.

How long we keep it

  • Audit log: 7 days on Hobbyist, 30 days on Tinkerer, and 90 days on Pro. Older entries are deleted automatically. Retention follows your current plan, so moving to a plan with a shorter period deletes older entries. On Enterprise, entries are kept until you delete the gateway or your account, unless your contract sets a period.
  • Account, gateways, servers, stored credentials, rules, pins, tokens, approvals, usage counts, and approval call records: until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Deleting a gateway deletes its servers, stored credentials, rules, tokens, approvals, and audit log. Deleting a server deletes its stored credential and pins.
  • Paired phone numbers: until you remove the number or your account is deleted. Unused pairing codes are deleted after about a day.
  • Plan and billing status: with your account, until it is deleted. Stripe keeps its own payment records under its privacy policy.
  • Sign-in sessions: 12 hours.
  • Hashed IP addresses: about 2 days.
  • Sales inquiries, and emails from the former waitlist: until you ask us to remove them.

Your choices and rights

  • In the app: delete gateways and servers, change rules, and revoke client tokens at any time.
  • By email: ask for a copy of your data, a correction, removal of a paired phone number, or full deletion of your account at privacy@fizzlayer.com. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, including California and the European Economic Area, you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete, or move your data, and to object to how we use it. We honor these requests for everyone, wherever you live. We will not treat you differently for using them.

Fizzlayer is run from the United States, so your data is processed there.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections. MCP server credentials are encrypted before they are stored, with a key kept outside the database. Passwords, session tokens, and registration tokens are stored only as hashes, client tokens are not stored, and only our servers can reach the database. No system is perfectly secure, though. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you.

Children

Fizzlayer is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes. For important changes, we will also tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
